Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a multiplayer game?

From the main menu, choose "Play with Friends" to share one card with your group, "Clash of the Teams" to compete as teams, or "Lone Wolves" to race head-to-head as individuals. You'll get a game code to share with other players.

How do I join someone's game?

Ask the game organizer for the game code (6 characters). Then choose the appropriate mode from the main menu and select "Join" to enter the code.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Solo Play works offline. The multiplayer modes — Play with Friends, Clash of the Teams, and Lone Wolves — require an internet connection for real-time sync.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription in your device settings: go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad.

How do I change the text on a bingo tile?

Before starting the game, long-press on any tile to edit its text. This lets you customize the card to your activity.

Can I create my own bingo card?

Yes! Choose "My Cards" from the main menu to create and save your own cards (up to 12). Add at least 28 clues to make a card playable, then pick the "Custom (My Cards)" category when starting a game. After a multiplayer game that used a custom card, other players can tap "Save This Card" on the completion screen to keep a copy in their own My Cards.

What is Treasure Hunt mode?

In Treasure Hunt, there's a hidden row or column you need to complete. Tiles on the correct path turn blue when marked. Tiles that are off the path turn gray. Use the blue tiles as clues to figure out the winning line!

I found a bug. How do I report it?

You can use the "Send Feedback" feature in the "About" section, or email us at getoutandgobingosupport@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your device type, and any steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are very helpful!
