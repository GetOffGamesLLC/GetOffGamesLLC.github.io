Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a multiplayer game?

From the main menu, choose "Play with Friends" to share a card with your group, or "Team Competition" to race against other teams. You'll get a game code to share with other players.

How do I join someone's game?

Ask the game organizer for the game code (6 characters). Then choose the appropriate mode from the main menu and select "Join" to enter the code.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Solo mode works offline. Multiplayer modes (Play with Friends and Team Competition) require an internet connection for real-time sync.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your device's settings. On iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play Store > your profile > Payments & subscriptions.

How do I change the text on a bingo tile?

Before starting the game, long-press on any tile to edit its text. This lets you customize the card to your activity.

What is Treasure Hunt mode?

In Treasure Hunt, there's a hidden row or column you need to complete. Tiles on the correct path turn blue when marked. Tiles that are off the path turn gray. Use the blue tiles as clues to figure out the winning line!

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email us at getoffgamesllc@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your device type, and any steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are very helpful!
