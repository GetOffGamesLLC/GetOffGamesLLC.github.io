Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

Get Off Games LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Real World Bingo mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Display Name: When you join or create a multiplayer game, you provide a display name. This is used solely to identify you within the game and is visible to other players in your game session.
  • Team Name: When you create a team, you provide a team name visible to other players in your competition.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Game Data: We store game session data (tile selections, game progress, scores) to enable real-time multiplayer gameplay. This data is temporary and is automatically deleted after games end.
  • Device Identifiers: We generate a random identifier on your device to manage your game sessions. This is not linked to your personal identity.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Real names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, or other personal files
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing or search history

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Enable real-time multiplayer gameplay
  • Display player and team names during games
  • Track game progress and scores within a session
  • Process in-app subscriptions through Apple and Google's payment systems

Data Storage and Security

Game data is stored using Google Firebase Realtime Database. Data is transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption. Game session data is temporary and is automatically cleaned up after games conclude.

Your display name and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers except during active game sessions.

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For real-time game data synchronization. Firebase's privacy policy can be found at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: For processing in-app subscription payments. We do not have access to your payment information.

Feedback Submissions

The App includes an optional feedback form. If you choose to submit feedback, you may provide your name and email address, both of which are optional. When feedback is submitted, the App also automatically collects your device model, iOS version, app version, and anonymous user ID to help us diagnose issues. Feedback data is stored on Firebase and retained until it is no longer needed for product improvement. If you include your name or email and would like it removed, you can contact us to request deletion.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App can be used by children under parental supervision. No account creation or personal information is required to play solo.

Data Retention

Game session data is automatically deleted after games end. Locally stored preferences (display name, team name, settings) remain on your device until you delete the App.

Your Rights

Since we collect minimal data and do not maintain user accounts, most data rights are handled automatically. You can:

  • Delete locally stored data by uninstalling the App
  • Choose any display name (it does not need to be your real name)
  • Contact us to request deletion of any game data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
