How to play

  1. Print the PDF on regular letter paper. Black and white works fine.
  2. Give everyone a different card and a pen.
  3. Mark each thing you spot. Five in a row, down a column or on a diagonal wins. Shout BINGO!

Play it live on your phones

In the Get Out & Go Bingo app, everyone plays on their own phone, the app checks every bingo, and you can drop bombs on your rivals' squares. New seasonal cards arrive through the holidays, and the first week is free.
