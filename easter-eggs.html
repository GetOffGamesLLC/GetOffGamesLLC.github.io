Easter Eggs

Get Out & Go Bingo has a few hidden surprises. Here's a riddle for each one. Stuck? Tap "Show me" for the answer, but try to find them yourself first!

Egg 1

The Ticklish Logo

Our logo is ticklish. Give it a few quick pokes and watch the menu go wild.

Show me

On the main menu, tap the big bingo logo 3 times quickly. The logo bounces and the letters on every menu button flip, one row at a time.

Egg 2

The Secret Stats

Good things come to those who wait. Be patient with the button that tells you all about us.

Show me

On the main menu, press and hold ABOUT for 3 seconds. You'll feel a buzz, then a hidden Secret Stats screen opens with your games played, total wins, current and best streak, and favorite category.

Egg 3

Shake It Up

Game going slowly? Shake things up.

Show me

While you're playing a game, shake your phone. Your bingo card gives a little wobble.

Egg 4

Holiday Confetti

Win on a holiday and see what falls from the sky.

Show me

Get a bingo on a holiday and the confetti changes to match: pumpkins and ghosts for Halloween (Oct 25–31), trees and snowflakes in December, fireworks for New Year's (Dec 31–Jan 2), hearts for Valentine's Day (Feb 10–14), and flags and eagles for the 4th of July (Jul 1–4).

Found them all? Show your friends, then challenge them to a game.
